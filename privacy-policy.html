Overview

K Studio creates mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our games or visit this website.

Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account on this website. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and message you choose to send.

Our games may use platform services such as Apple App Store purchases, TestFlight, analytics, or advertising services. These services may process technical information such as device type, app version, crash data, purchase status, advertising identifiers, or approximate usage information according to their own policies.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Advertising

Some games may display ads through third-party advertising services. These services may use device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, limit, or measure ads, depending on your device settings and consent choices.

Children

Our games are intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Data Retention

Support emails may be kept as long as needed to respond and manage support requests. Data handled by Apple or third-party services is retained according to their policies.

Your Choices

You can manage app permissions, tracking preferences, subscriptions, and purchases in your device or Apple account settings.
